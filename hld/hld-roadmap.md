This curriculum is designed to transition a postgraduate student from academic theoretical knowledge to the practical, high-pressure decision-making required in FAANG system design interviews. It follows a "First Principles to Application" pedagogy, ensuring that when a student designs a complex system like Netflix, they are making decisions based on deep architectural trade-offs rather than rote memorization.

### Key Success Factors

1.  **Trade-off Centricity:** Never provide a "single right answer." In every design, explicitly state: *"I chose X over Y because of [Reasoning], acknowledging the drawback of [Trade-off]."*
2.  **Estimation Fluency:** You must be able to quickly translate "100 million users" into "Requests Per Second," "Storage required per year," and "Bandwidth requirements." This anchors your design in reality.
3.  **Diagramming Precision:** Use standard notation (Load Balancers, Databases, Cache layers) in tools like Excalidraw. Your diagrams should be clean enough for a senior engineer to follow without verbal explanation.
4.  **The "Deep Dive" Capability:** FAANG interviewers will pick one component (e.g., "How exactly does your database handle a node failure?") and drill down. You must move from the "Bird's Eye View" to the "Microscopic View" seamlessly.
